Common questions

Mizo tawngin thil pawimawh te hi hetah chhiar theih a ni.

NIOS admission tan kum engzat atangin nge apply theih?

Secondary tan minimum age 14 a ni. Senior Secondary tan minimum age 15 a ni. Qualification leh required documents penuhi tur.

Secondary admission tan qualification eng nge mamawh?

Class VIII pass emaw self-certificate hmangin Secondary admission eligibility nei theih a ni. Date of Birth proof pawh upload tur.

Senior Secondary admission tan qualification eng nge mamawh?

Secondary Examination recognized Board atanga pass tawh tur a ni. Senior Secondary-ah direct admission without Secondary qualification chu tih theih a ni lo.

NIOS admission hi kum tluanin tih theih a ni em?

Normal online admission Stream 1-ah Block I leh Block II a awm. Stream 3 Secondary ODE leh Stream 4 Senior Secondary ODE chu prospectus-a sawi angin open throughout the year.

Stream 1 chu eng nge?

Stream 1 chu normal online admission route a ni. Block I admission chu March–April 2027 exam atan, Block II admission chu September–October 2027 exam atan a ni.

Stream 2 chu eng nge?

Stream 2 hi immediate Public Examination route a ni. Secondary pass tawh learner-te subject 1–4 Part Admission atan emaw prospectus-a specified failed/could-not-appear cases atan hman theih a ni.

ODE chu eng nge?

ODE chu On Demand Examination a ni. Learner-in exam pe thei hunah eligibility, subject, designated centre leh seat availability a zirah apply theih a ni. Immediate exam guarantee a ni lo.

ODE exam chu khawiah nge tih?

ODE exam chu NIOS designated examination centre-ah tih tur a ni. Prospectus-ah NIOS A-31 Institutional Area, Sector 62, Noida leh designated Kendriya Vidyalayas across India te an sawi.

NIOS admission fee engzat nge?

Fee chu course, subject number leh learner category-in a danglam.Exact fee i category leh subject number sawi chuan Assistant-in check pui thei.

Exam fee leh admission fee hi a inang em?

A inang lo. Admission fee chu admission hunah pek tur a ni. Public Examination fee chu exam registration hunah separate-in pek tur a ni.

Public Exam fee engzat nge?

Public Examination theory fee chu ₹300 per subject a ni. Practical subject-ah practical fee additional ₹150 per subject a ni.

NIOS Public Exam hi kum khatah vawi engzat nge?

Public Examination chu kum khatah vawi 2, March–April leh September–October session-ah a awm.

Senior Secondary-ah subject engzat nge kan lak theih?

Certification atan minimum 5 subjects lak tur a ni, Group A language 1 emaw 2 telin. Additional subject pawh prospectus-a rule angin lak theih a ni, maximum 7 subjects thleng.

Subject combination ka duh danin ka thlang thei em?

Subject combination hi target course leh NIOS grouping rules te enin thlang tur a ni. University/course requirement pawh check hmasa tur.

Senior Secondary-ah 2-year gap eng nge a awmzia?

Senior Secondary certificate dawn turin Secondary pass year atanga essential 2-year gap complete tur a ni. Gap complete loh chuan forthcoming exam-ah subject 4 aia tam exam tih theih loh a awm.

TOC chu eng nge?

TOC chu Transfer of Credit a ni. Recognised Board ex-Secondary/Senior Secondary learner, subject pakhat aia tam pass tawh mahse overall qualify lo learner-te tan maximum 2 subjects thlengin prospectus rule angin hman theih a ni.

Part Admission chu eng nge?

Part Admission hmangin learner-in subject 1–4 thleng lak theih a ni. Passing hnuah Marksheet chauh pek a ni; certificate dang pek a ni lo.

NIOS learner-te tan Study Centre-ah class an kal ngai em?

NIOS chu Open and Distance Learning system a ni. Study Centre-ah PCP, practical session, counselling leh learner support services pek thin a ni. Self-Learning Material pawh learner-in self-study atan hman tur.

PCP chu eng nge?

PCP chu Personal Contact Programme a ni. Subject tinah induction leh theory PCP sessions an awm, practical subject-ah practical sessions pawh a awm.

TMA chu eng nge?

TMA chu Tutor Marked Assignment a ni. Stream 1 learner-te tan applicable subject tinah assignment a awm a, Internal Assessment-ah 20% theory marks a tel.

TMA hi Stream 2, 3 leh 4-ah submit tur a ni em?

Ni lo. TMA chu Stream 1 learner-te tan a ni. Stream 2, Stream 3 leh Stream 4-ah TMA applicable a ni lo.

NIOS certificate hi university-ah hman theih em?

NIOS Prospectus-in higher education leh other purposes atan certificate acceptance a sawi. Mahse university/course tinin eligibility criteria an hran theuh thei, chuvangin target institution requirement check tur.

NIOS certificate hi Government job-ah hman theih em?

Prospectus-ah NIOS certificates chu Government jobs leh other purposes atan accepted a ni tih sawi a ni. Specific post/service rules chu concerned recruiting authority nen check tur.

Exam centre ka duh ber ka thlang thei em?

Exam centre chu NIOS-in allot a ni. AI/Study Centre-in particular examination centre guarantee thei lo.

Exam atan eng documents nge mamawh?

Public Examination atan valid NIOS Identity Card mamawh a ni. Hall Ticket pawh check tur. ODE atan original NIOS I-card, valid photo ID leh Hall Ticket keng tur.

Result-ah error ka hmu chuan engtin nge tih tur?

Public/ODE result discrepancy awm chuan result declaration atanga ni 30 chhungin concerned Regional Director-ah application submit tur. Ni 30 liam hnuah application chu entertain loh a ni.

Result rechecking leh re-evaluation hi tih theih em?

Aw. Rechecking leh re-evaluation provision a awm. Rechecking fee ₹400 per subject a ni. Re-evaluation fee chu Public Examination-ah ₹1000 per subject, ODE-ah ₹1200 per subject a ni. Theory paper atan chauh applicable a ni.

NIOS admission chu online-in tih theih em?

Aw. Admission chu 100% online-in tih theih a ni. Learner-in NIOS website, AI/Study Centre, Regional Centre emaw CSC hmangin application submit theih a ni.
