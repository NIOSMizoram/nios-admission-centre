Common questions

Mizo tawngin thil pawimawh te hi hetah chhiar theih a ni.

NIOS admission tan kum engzat atangin nge apply theih?

Secondary tan minimum age 14 a ni. Senior Secondary tan minimum age 15 a ni. Qualification leh document requirement pawh penuhi tur.

Stream 1 chu eng nge?

Stream 1 chu normal online admission route a ni. Eligibility condition te penuhi learner-te tan hman tur a ni.

Senior Secondary fee engzat nge?

Prospectus 2026–27 fee table-ah 5 subjects tan category hrang hrangin fee a hran. Chatbot-ah current official fee te zawt theih a ni. Centre margin/service charge hi official NIOS fee-ah tel lo.

ODE chu exam rang tak tih theihna em?

ODE chu On Demand Examination a ni. Mahse eligibility, subject, designated centre leh seat availability check hmasa tur. Immediate exam guarantee a ni lo.

NIOS certificate hi university-ah hman theih em?

NIOS Prospectus-in higher education leh other purposes tan certificate acceptance a sawi. Mahse university/course tinin eligibility criteria an hran theuh thei, chuvangin target institution requirement check tur.
